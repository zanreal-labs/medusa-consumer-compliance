---
title: Checkout consents
description: Consents the customer gives at checkout, chosen by the cart contents, enforced server-side when the cart is completed, and frozen verbatim onto the order as proof.
---

Some orders cannot be placed without a consent. A store that sells licence keys
(ESD codes) delivers them at once, and the right of withdrawal only disappears
if the customer expressly consented to that and acknowledged losing the right
(art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 13 of the consumer rights act). We do not accept such an
order without that consent, just as Allegro does not. Other consents, such as
accepting the terms, may apply to every order.

## Definitions

**Settings > Consumer compliance > Checkout consents** holds the definitions:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Key | Stable identifier sent by the frontend, for example `digital_immediate_delivery`. Never changes. |
| Text (PL, EN) | Exactly the words the customer sees and accepts. |
| Linked document | Optional URL and link text, for example the terms. |
| Required | A required consent blocks completing the cart until accepted. |
| Active | Inactive consents are never shown. |
| Purpose | `digital_content_withdrawal_waiver` marks the art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 13 consent; accepting it is what lifts the right of withdrawal for the lines it covered. |
| Rules | When the consent applies to a cart (below). |

Changing the text or the link creates a **new version**. Earlier versions are
kept with their text, and so is every acceptance. When a document is linked,
the plugin downloads it on save and stores its SHA-256 with the version, so an
order can show which version of the terms was in force. A document that cannot
be fetched is saved without a hash.

### The seeded consent

On first use the plugin creates `digital_immediate_delivery`, required, matching
products whose metadata has `is_digital_content: true`:

> Wyrażam zgodę na rozpoczęcie dostarczania treści cyfrowej (kodu) przed upływem
> terminu do odstąpienia od umowy. Przyjmuję do wiadomości, że po dostarczeniu
> treści cyfrowej utracę prawo odstąpienia od umowy.

> I consent to the supply of the digital content (code) starting before the
> withdrawal period ends. I acknowledge that once the digital content has been
> supplied, I lose my right to withdraw from the contract.

The first sentence is the express and prior consent, the second the
acknowledgement, the two elements art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 13 requires. It is created
once; editing or deleting it later is respected.

## Rules

A consent applies to a cart when **any** of its rules matches at least one line:

| Rule | Matches a line when |
| --- | --- |
| `always` | Always (every cart). |
| `product_flag` | The product's metadata key is set (`true`, `1`, `"true"`, `"yes"`, `"tak"`). |
| `product` | The product id is listed. |
| `product_type` | The product type id is listed. |
| `product_tag` | One of the product's tag ids is listed. |
| `collection` | The collection id is listed. |
| `category` | One of the product's category ids is listed. |

A definition with no rules is never shown. For the digital consent the matched
lines matter: in a mixed cart only those products lose the right of withdrawal.

## Enforcement

Enforcement is on the server, in the `validate` hook of Medusa's
`completeCartWorkflow`, so `POST /store/carts/:id/complete` fails before anything
is reserved or charged when a required consent that applies to the cart is
missing (`consent_required`) or was accepted in an older version
(`consent_version_outdated`). The frontend cannot skip it. Medusa allows one
handler per workflow hook, so no other code in the backend may register
`completeCartWorkflow.hooks.validate`; today nothing else does.

## Snapshot on the order

When the cart becomes an order (`order.placed`), the plugin freezes every
accepted consent onto the order in its own table: the full text as rendered,
the locale, the version, whether it was required, the linked document URL and
its hash, the time, and the client's IP and user agent. These rows cannot be
changed or deleted, so editing a definition later never changes what an order
shows. For the digital consent it also writes `digital_content_consent_at` and
`digital_content_consent_product_ids` to the order metadata.

The order page in the admin shows them in the **Zgody klienta** widget, verbatim.
`GET /admin/consumer-compliance/orders/:id/consents` returns them for one order,
and `GET /admin/consumer-compliance/order-consents?format=csv` exports them. The
withdrawal acknowledgement e-mail quotes them.

**Confirmation on a durable medium.** Art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 13 also requires the
trader to confirm the consent on a durable medium (art. 21 ust. 1). The order
confirmation e-mail, sent by the backend, should quote the accepted consent; the
snapshot gives it the exact text.

## Allegro and other marketplaces

Allegro collects its own consents and runs its own withdrawal process. Orders
imported by medusa-allegro are created with `createOrderWorkflow`, not through a
cart, so the complete-cart enforcement never runs for them. The plugin
recognises them by `metadata.allegro_checkout_form_id` (or by a sales channel
in `externalSalesChannelIds`) and leaves them alone: no snapshot, no metadata
write, no missing-consent flag; the admin widget says "Zgody zbierane przez
Allegro" and the withdrawal lookup answers `external_marketplace_order`.
