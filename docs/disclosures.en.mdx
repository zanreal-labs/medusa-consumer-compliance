---
title: Reviews and personalised pricing
description: The two Omnibus disclosures a shop must make in its own words, edited in the admin and served by the Store API.
---

Both are texts only the store can write, so the plugin stores and serves them; it
is not a review system.

## Reviews

A shop that gives access to consumer reviews must say **whether and how** it
ensures they come from consumers who used or bought the product (art. 6 ust. 4
pkt 7 of the Polish unfair market practices act, art. 7(6) of Directive
2005/29/EC). Claiming reviews are verified without reasonable steps to check, and
publishing or distorting reviews, are blacklisted (art. 7 pkt 25 and 26).

Write the text in **Settings > Consumer compliance > Disclosures**, in plain
language: who can post, whether purchase is checked and how, whether all reviews
are published, how moderation works, whether any were incentivised. Show it
directly next to the reviews block.

## Personalised pricing

If prices are individually adjusted on the basis of automated decision-making,
the consumer must be told before ordering (art. 12 ust. 1 pkt 5a of the consumer
rights act, art. 6(1)(ea) of Directive 2011/83/EU). Dynamic pricing that does not
personalise is not covered. Switch on "prices are personalised" and write the
notice; the Store API returns `personalised_pricing.used` and the text.

## Optional texts

- **Withdrawal info**, shown next to the withdrawal button (for example where to
  send goods back).
- **Omnibus note**, shown with reduced prices (for example in a tooltip).

All texts are localised and come back as `null` when not filled in, so the
frontend can render nothing rather than an empty heading.
