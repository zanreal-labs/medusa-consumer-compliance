title: Withdrawal from the contract
description: The online withdrawal function of Directive 2023/2673 as a two-step Store API flow, the 14-day period computed by Polish rules, statutory exceptions, the acknowledgement on a durable medium and the model withdrawal form.
---

A consumer may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days without giving a
reason (art. 27 of the Polish consumer rights act). Directive (EU) 2023/2673 adds
art. 11a to Directive 2011/83/EU: a shop that concludes contracts online must
offer a **withdrawal function** on its online interface. It applies from 19 June
2026.

> **Status in Poland (19 October 2026).** The Polish implementing act is not in
> force. The government draft UC169 (amending the consumer rights act with new
> art. 30a and 30b) was sent to consultation on 10 September 2026 and would enter
> into force 30 days after publication, covering withdrawal periods still running
> at that date. The flow below follows the directive and the draft. See
> [Legal basis](./legal.en.mdx).

## The flow

```text
consumer                         storefront                         plugin
--------                         ----------                         ------
"Odstąp od umowy tutaj"  ──>  lookup (order + e-mail, or logged in) ──> items, eligibility, deadlines
fills name, items, e-mail ──>  POST /withdrawals                     ──> record: pending_confirmation, token
"Potwierdź odstąpienie   ──>  POST /withdrawals/:id/confirm (token)  ──> record: confirmed, submitted_at
 od umowy"                                                              event + acknowledgement e-mail
```

**Step one** records the statement: the consumer's name, the contract (order and
items), and the e-mail for the confirmation (the three elements of art. 11a(2)).
Nothing is submitted yet. The response carries a one-time `confirmation_token`
(only its hash is stored), valid for `confirmationTtlMinutes` (60 by default).

**Step two** is the confirmation button. Its moment is the legal submission time,
stored as `submitted_at`; sending the statement before the period ends is enough
(art. 11a(5), art. 30 ust. 2 of the act). The plugin then emits
`consumer_compliance.withdrawal.confirmed` and sends the acknowledgement.

A guest identifies the order by its number and the order e-mail; a logged-in
customer can use any of their orders. A wrong e-mail and an unknown order give the
same 404, so the lookup cannot probe order numbers. Put the routes behind your rate
limiter.

## Labels

| Element | Polish | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry point (on the element that opens the function) | Odstąp od umowy tutaj | Withdraw from contract here |
| Confirmation button | Potwierdź odstąpienie od umowy | Confirm withdrawal |

Both come from the Polish text of the directive and the draft act, and are
returned by `GET /store/consumer-compliance/disclosures` as `withdrawal.labels`.
The draft's explanatory memorandum adds: the label sits on the button itself, the
consumer can pick items, pre-filled data must be editable, and the consumer must
not be redirected to another page.

## The period

Per item, by Polish rules:

- **goods**: 14 days from taking possession; for several goods delivered
  separately, from the last delivery (art. 28 pkt 1). The plugin reads Medusa
  fulfillments marked delivered. Until every shipment is delivered, the period has
  not started and the function stays available;
- **items without shipping** (digital content, services): 14 days from the
  conclusion of the contract, the order date (art. 28 pkt 2);
- the day of the event is not counted (art. 111 par. 2 of the Civil Code) and the
  period ends at 23:59:59 Polish time on its last day;
- a last day on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday moves to the next working day
  (art. 115 of the Civil Code; UOKiK confirms this applies). Christmas Eve is a
  public holiday from 2025. Set `extendOverNonWorkingDays: false` to switch it off.

A statement submitted after the period is still accepted and marked
`within_period: false`, because the trader has to assess it (the extension to 12
months under art. 29 is one reason). `rejectOutsidePeriod: true` refuses it
instead.

## Exceptions (art. 38 ust. 1)

Each product can carry one statutory exception: custom-made, perishable, sealed
hygiene goods, inseparably mixed, alcohol with a market-dependent price, sealed
recordings or software, periodicals, digital content. Most exclude the item
outright. Two need facts the shop cannot see:

- **sealed goods** are excluded only once unsealed after delivery, so they stay
  selectable and the trader decides;
- **digital content** is excluded only if the consumer consented to immediate
  supply and acknowledged losing the right. Store that moment as an ISO date in
  the order metadata key `digital_content_consent_at` (configurable) when the
  checkout collects the consent.

## The acknowledgement

Art. 11a(4) requires an acknowledgement on a durable medium, without undue delay,
with the statement's content and the date and time of submission. The plugin sends
it through the Medusa notification module on the `email` channel with ready
`content` (subject, HTML and text, Polish or English) plus `template` and `data`
for providers that render their own. If sending fails, the withdrawal stays
submitted, the error is stored on the record, and the admin can resend it.

## The admin register

**Withdrawals** in the sidebar lists every submitted statement with its deadline,
acknowledgement status and items. A statement can be marked processed or
rejected with an internal note, and the order page shows its withdrawals.

## Model withdrawal form

The consumer may also use the model form of załącznik nr 2 to the consumer rights
act (t.j. Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1244), which the order confirmation should carry.
Current wording:

```text
WZÓR FORMULARZA ODSTĄPIENIA OD UMOWY
(formularz ten należy wypełnić i odesłać tylko w przypadku chęci odstąpienia od umowy)

- Adresat [w tym miejscu przedsiębiorca powinien wpisać nazwę przedsiębiorcy,
  pełny adres pocztowy oraz adres e-mail]
- Ja/My(*) niniejszym informuję/informujemy(*) o moim/naszym odstąpieniu od umowy
  sprzedaży następujących towarów(*) umowy dostawy następujących towarów(*)
  umowy o dzieło polegającej na wykonaniu następujących towarów(*)/o świadczenie
  następującej usługi(*)
- Data zawarcia umowy(*)/odbioru(*)
- Imię i nazwisko konsumenta(-ów)
- Adres konsumenta(-ów)
- Podpis konsumenta(-ów) (tylko jeżeli formularz jest przesyłany w wersji papierowej)
- Data

(*) Niepotrzebne skreślić.
```

Once the Polish act passes, the instructions (załącznik nr 1, note 3) gain a
sentence about withdrawing online; the draft wording is quoted in
[Legal basis](./legal.en.mdx).
