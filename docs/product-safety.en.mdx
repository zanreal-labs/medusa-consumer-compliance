---
title: Product safety (GPSR)
description: The information art. 19 of the General Product Safety Regulation requires in every online offer, kept as reusable manufacturer and responsible person records, with Polish warnings and a list of what is missing.
---

Since 13 December 2024, every online offer of a consumer product must show
clearly and visibly (art. 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988):

1. the manufacturer's name, registered trade name or trade mark, and its postal
   and electronic address;
2. if the manufacturer is not established in the EU, the same for the responsible
   person in the EU;
3. information identifying the product, including a picture, its type and any
   other identifier;
4. warnings and safety information, in a language consumers easily understand.

In Poland that language is Polish (art. 4 of the act of 7 November 2025 on
supervision of general product safety, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1826, in force since
3 January 2026). A missing art. 19 element can cost up to 500 000 zł (art. 76).

## Economic operators

Manufacturers, EU responsible persons and importers are kept once
(**Settings > Consumer compliance > Manufacturers**) and assigned to products: a
catalogue usually has a few brands and many products. An operator has a name,
trade name, postal address, country code, e-mail, website and phone. The country
decides whether a responsible person in the EU is required. An operator still
assigned to products cannot be deleted.

## Product data

On the product page, the compliance card holds:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Not covered by GPSR | For things that are not consumer products in the GPSR sense, such as a licence key or a service. Switches all checks off. |
| Manufacturer, responsible person | Picked from the operators. |
| Product identifier | Type, model, batch or serial number. Variant SKUs and barcodes (EAN, UPC) are added automatically. |
| Warnings, safety information | Localised, Polish first. |
| No warnings required | An explicit statement, so an empty field is never mistaken for a decision. |
| Label and instruction images | URLs, one per line. |
| Accessibility notes | Localised text alternative for labels or instructions shown as images (see below). |

## Validation

The card shows what is missing, and **Settings > Consumer compliance > GPSR gaps**
lists every published product with gaps. The checks:

- a manufacturer with a name or trade name, a postal address, an e-mail or
  website, and a country;
- a responsible person when the manufacturer is outside the EU, itself with an
  address and contact and established in the EU;
- an identifier (the product's own, or a variant SKU or barcode) and an image;
- warnings or safety information in Polish, unless the product is marked as
  needing none.

Validation never blocks a save: a half-filled record is better than none.

## Accessibility (European Accessibility Act)

The Polish accessibility act (Dz.U. 2024 poz. 731) applies to e-commerce services
from 28 June 2025, except services of microenterprises (fewer than 10 people and
at most EUR 2 million turnover or balance sheet). Almost all of it is storefront
work: WCAG 2.1 AA in identification, checkout, payment and the withdrawal
function, and accessibility information in the terms. What a backend can add is
data: the accessibility notes field above, product identifiers in text rather than
only in images, and acknowledgement e-mails with a plain-text part (the plugin
sends both HTML and text).
